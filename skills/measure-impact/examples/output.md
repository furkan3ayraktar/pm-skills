# [DRAFT] Guided Onboarding Impact Plan (Illustrative)

**tl;dr:** Test whether guided onboarding helps more new teams complete their first shared task within a week, with manageable support demand, before expanding it.

## 1. Hypothesis

Guiding workspace owners through inviting a teammate will help more teams complete their first shared task within seven days, because the next step is clearer.

## 2. How We Will Measure Impact

Figures are illustrative. Compare both measures with current onboarding.

| Measurement | Why it matters | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **7-day teammate task completion rate**<br>Share of assigned workspaces where an invited teammate joins and completes a task. | Teams start working together | At least 5 percentage points higher (e.g. 31% to 36%) | Less than 5 points higher: below target |
| 2. **7-day onboarding support contacts per workspace**<br>Total onboarding support contacts divided by all assigned workspaces. | Checks whether setup needs more help | No more than 10% higher | More than 10% higher: too much added support |

## 3. How We Will Test the Hypothesis

1. **Prepare the data for measurements 1 and 2 before launch.** Link dated records of workspace creation, onboarding version, teammate joins, task completions, and support contacts by workspace and member, then confirm baselines, targets, and the required test size.

2. **Give new customers one of two onboarding versions.** Randomly assign each customer's first workspace to guided or current onboarding, excluding staff and test accounts beforehand.

3. **Measure every workspace after seven days.** Count everyone assigned, including those who never open the guide.

4. **Expand only if measurement 1 improves by at least five percentage points and measurement 2 rises by no more than 10%.** If a target is clearly missed, revise the guide or address the extra support demand before expanding.

5. **Collect more evidence if the result is unclear.** Fix missing or unreliable records and complete the agreed test size before deciding whether the targets were met.
